---

## 🎯 Strategic Philosophy & Vision

This repository is a **25-Stage Progressive Research Journey** mapping the evolution from foundational computer architecture and networking to elite vulnerability research, offensive tradecraft, binary exploitation, digital forensics, and detection engineering.

Every research topic and CTF writeup adheres strictly to the **Dual-Layer Mastermind Methodology**:
1. **The Technical Layer**: Objective, Environment, Tool Mechanics, Syntaxes, Telemetry Output, and Defensive Mitigations.
2. **The Human Research Layer**: Initial Hypothesis, Failure Analysis, The Analytical Pivot, and Defensive Lessons.
